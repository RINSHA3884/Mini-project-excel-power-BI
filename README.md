# Sales Performance Analysis | Excel & Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing sales data using **Microsoft Excel** and **Microsoft Power BI**. The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, preparation, visualisation, and interactive dashboard development.

The analysis explores revenue, product categories, customer segments, geographical sales performance, payment methods, shipping modes, and monthly sales trends.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel – Data inspection, cleaning, and preparation
- Microsoft Power BI – Data visualisation, KPI cards, and interactive dashboard development
- Microsoft Word – Project documentation

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning and Preparation – Excel

The initial dataset was inspected and prepared using Microsoft Excel.

Activities included:
- Reviewing the dataset structure and columns.
- Identifying blank and missing values.
- Removing selected rows containing missing values where appropriate.
- Checking data consistency.
- Reviewing and adjusting data types and formatting.
- Saving the cleaned dataset for Power BI.

### 2. Dashboard Development – Power BI

The cleaned dataset was imported into Power BI to develop an interactive sales performance dashboard.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**

| KPI | Reported Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | 1.04 Million |
| Total Quantity | Approximately 55K |
| Total Orders | Approximately 1K |
| Average Revenue per Invoice | Approximately 1.04K |

### 3. Data Visualisations

The dashboard includes the following analyses:

- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Payment Mode
- Revenue by Customer Segment
- Revenue by Country
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Quantity by Shipping Mode
- Revenue by Sales Zone
- Quantity by Sub-Category

### 4. Interactive Features

The dashboard includes:

- **Slicers:** Category, Ship Mode, Country, and Segment.
- **Drill-Down:** Country → City → Category → Product Name.
- **Drill-Through:** A separate Category Details page for exploring category-level information.

## Key Findings

- Office Supplies generated the highest revenue among the product categories.
- The East sales zone recorded the highest revenue.
- Consumer customers contributed the largest reported share of revenue.
- Credit card and check payments accounted for the largest reported revenue shares.
- Monthly revenue peaks were observed in June, August, and September.
- Storage, Art, and Binders were among the leading sub-categories by quantity.
- Economy shipping recorded the highest quantity.

## Business Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing product categories.
- Explore opportunities to improve sales in lower-performing categories.
- Strengthen customer engagement across different segments.
- Use geographical sales trends to support regional planning.
- Consider monthly revenue patterns when planning promotions.
- Monitor product demand and shipping preferences to improve operational efficiency.
